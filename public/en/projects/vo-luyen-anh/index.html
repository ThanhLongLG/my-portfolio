Luyện Anh.

A local-first IELTS Academic self-study assistant that turns Reading, Vocabulary, Writing, and Grammar into an evidence-based adaptive learning loop.

01 / OVERVIEW

More than knowing
how much you studied.

Vở Luyện Anh prioritises capability over completed-exercise counts. Scores, errors, mastery, and review schedules are stored as structured data; AI creates content and feedback but never replaces the core learning logic.

Within the MVP, the app serves one learner on a personal computer and requires no account. Learning data remains local, and AI content must be validated before it is shown.

02 / LEARNING LOOP

Learn, reuse,
and prove capability.

  1. 01

    Set the goal

    Configure an AI provider, target band, study schedule, and budget before starting.

  2. 02

    Initial assessment

    Measure Reading, Grammar, Vocabulary, and Writing to build the first learner profile.

  3. 03

    Choose a topic

    The path unlocks in a fixed order, beginning with Education, while difficulty can adapt.

  4. 04

    Guided Reading

    AI generates and validates an exercise; the learner receives answers, explanations, and passage evidence.

  5. 05

    Active Vocabulary

    Vocabulary from Reading, Writing, and personal errors is suggested for learning, practice, and review.

  6. 06

    Writing & revision

    The learner uses studied vocabulary, gets evidence-based feedback, revises, and resubmits.

  7. 07

    Update capability

    Results are stored structurally to update mastery, weaknesses, and the next review schedule.

  8. 08

    Review on time

    Vocabulary and Grammar return through SRS and evidence of actual weaknesses.

04 / ACTIVE LEARNING

Vocabulary moves
into real writing.

Vocabulary is more than flashcards. Each learning item contains contextual meaning, pronunciation, examples, word families, collocations, and a set of around 10 exercises. At topic vocabulary milestones, the app proposes Writing so learners reuse the words in context.

  • At most five new vocabulary items are suggested each day to avoid overload.
  • The first exercise needs at least 8/10, including a successful self-written sentence.
  • SRS returns items at 1, 3, 7, 14, and 30 days.
  • Writing is assessed against four IELTS criteria and encourages revision before showing a model.

05 / SYSTEM DESIGN

01

Validated AI

New content passes JSON-schema generation, rule checks, and independent validation before display.

02

Local-first & safe

Learning data lives in SQLite; API keys stay in the operating system keyring, never in the database, logs, or backups.

03

Continuous offline

AI tasks queue for retry; learners can still use saved content, view progress, and keep writing when offline.
